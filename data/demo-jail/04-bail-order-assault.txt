ORDER

This is the second bail application moved on behalf of the applicant G— K— seeking his release on bail in the aforesaid case.

Briefly stated, the prosecution case is that on 4 September 2023, over a land dispute, the applicant abused the informant D— K—, beat him with sticks and threatened to kill him. The FIR was lodged on 05.09.2023 at P.S. Hariharganj.

Learned counsel for the applicant submitted that the applicant is innocent and has been falsely implicated due to enmity. It is further submitted that investigation is complete and the charge-sheet has been filed, hence no useful purpose would be served by keeping the applicant in jail. It is also submitted that the FIR is delayed without any plausible explanation. It is also submitted that there is no likelihood of the trial concluding in the near future. The applicant has been languishing in jail for long and the trial is getting delayed. If released on bail, the applicant shall abide by all conditions.

Per contra, learned D.G.C. (Criminal) for the State opposed the bail application and submitted that the applicant is named in the FIR and, if released, he may influence the witnesses.

I have heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the record. The charge-sheet has been filed and the trial is pending at the stage of evidence. Considering the nature and gravity of the offence, and without expressing any opinion on the merits of the case, I do not find sufficient ground to release the applicant on bail.

ORDER
The second bail application of the applicant G— K— is rejected. The trial court is expected to conclude the trial expeditiously.
